# Reviewer Positioning Snippets

## Selective Prediction Reviewer

We agree that reachability is closely related to class-conditional coverage trajectories and selective-prediction threshold analysis. Our claim is not a new mathematical object. The manuscript contribution is a severe-imbalance, minority-conditioned operational reinterpretation that integrates trajectory shape with persistence, jump, and smoothness diagnostics to assess threshold-policy controllability in deployment-like settings.

## Calibration Reviewer

This manuscript does not challenge calibration and does not introduce a calibration method. We use standard calibration tools and treat reliability as important. The specific contribution is an interaction analysis under severe imbalance: in this benchmark slice, reliability improvements can coexist with less smooth accessibility trajectories, so calibration quality and accessibility controllability should be interpreted jointly rather than as interchangeable signals.

## Imbalance Reviewer

The work is intentionally grounded in established imbalance practice (cost-sensitive framing, weighting, oversampling, HDDT-family baselines). We do not claim learner novelty. The added value is evaluative: under severe imbalance, models with similar ranking summaries can still diverge in threshold-mediated minority accessibility behavior, which is operationally relevant when thresholds are adjusted in practice.

## Operational ML Reviewer

The manuscript is positioned as deployment-behavior analysis for threshold-mediated systems (triage, fraud review, sparse-event monitoring). Instead of treating threshold choice as a one-time optimization, we evaluate how accessibility changes as policy thresholds move. This trajectory-centric view provides an interpretable controllability signal for operational teams while remaining conservative about external validity beyond the current dataset slice.
